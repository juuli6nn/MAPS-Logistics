About Us

Built on reliability,
driven by commitment.

In the new post-pandemic age, global trade and local commerce are thriving, and businesses are constantly exchanging commodities. To ensure smooth operations, an efficient trucking partner is essential for the final leg of the supply chain. MAPS LOGISTICS SERVICES is here to provide that crucial support. We specialize in offering dependable, last-mile transportation solutions that meet the evolving needs of modern businesses. Whether you're a retailer, manufacturer, or distributor, we are your trusted partner to ensure timely and secure delivery of goods.

MAPS LOGISTICS SERVICES was founded in 2023, with a vision to offer reliable, efficient trucking services that meet the growing demands of a dynamic marketplace. From humble beginnings, we have expanded our fleet and service offerings to become a leading player in the trucking and logistics industry. Over the years, we have built a reputation for excellence, expanding our reach to meet the logistics needs of businesses across diverse sectors.
